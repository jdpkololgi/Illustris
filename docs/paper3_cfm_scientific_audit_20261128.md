# Paper III scientific audit and publication decision

28 November 2026 · frozen seed-17 model · repository revision `9461304`

**Decision 4. Publish the field reconstruction and its validation in an astronomy journal. Present the dependence experiment as a non-archival ML-for-physical-sciences workshop paper.** RASTI is my first choice for the current methods-and-data manuscript. The Paper III held-out follow-up makes the decision clearer. Once a simple template supplies spatial ranks to the same cellwise predictions, the large advantage over independent shuffling largely disappears. One held-out phase favors each coupling at the primary separation. The evidence establishes the scientific cost of discarding dependence and the value of testing alternatives that preserve marginals. It does not yet establish a generally better learned coupling, or a reproducible failure serious enough to warrant a negative-results main-track paper.

**Two-sentence ML contribution.** We construct observation-conditioned, mass-conserving survey-cap matter realizations and test their induced uncertainty on a nonlinear measurement of the cosmic web while holding every cell’s empirical predictions fixed. Independent cellwise resampling systematically understates short-range filament association, whereas a training-region rank template recovers much of the coherent sampler’s skill, exposing how strongly the apparent advantage depends on the choice of dependence control.

**Astronomy contribution.** Reusable draws of R7 density and its derived tidal web in both DESI Loa BGS_BRIGHT caps allow an observer to propagate reconstruction uncertainty through a specified measurement. Matched full-box mock tests quantify what can be trusted at individual cells, what changes at the regional level, and where survey transfer remains uncertain.

### How the field is made

The factorization is a sampled coarse density given survey observations, then a fine residual given those observations and that coarse draw, followed by exterior completion and a deterministic tidal operator given the completed density. Training supplies the true coarse field to the fine factor. Inference supplies an aligned crop of the sampled coarse field, a possible train–inference mismatch. Coarse log-density is centered and standardized using training values. The fine latent is the within-block mean-subtracted log density divided by a training SD. For a block of 64 fine cells with sampled mean density C, the decoded density is 64 C times a within-block softmax. The cell density is positive and the block mean is exactly C up to roundoff. `e2e_vdm_context_models.py` lines 37–52, `e2e_wholefield_pilot_train.py` lines 41–67.

Both local and wide observations enter the coarse factor, including spatial encodings at the bottleneck (`e2e_coupled_benchmark_models.py` lines 87–134). The native local condition contains counts, random support, angular response, apodized exposure, expected counts, log count ratio, distance from support boundary, radial selection density, three line-of-sight components and observer redshift. Wide channels include aggregated counts and expected counts, support, response, exposure, radial selection, valid geometry fraction, log count ratio, line of sight and observer radius. See `e2e_coupled_condition_products.py` lines 21–29 for the stored order and `lowz_ph006_cap_draw.py` lines 39–76 for routing. These are survey-conditioned fields, with fixed simulation and galaxy-population priors. The neural field target is density. Ordered eigenvalues are derived by diagonalization. The per-galaxy ordered-increment head from Paper II does not enter this chain.

Each stage starts with its own cap-wide Gaussian noise. The coarse 48³ windows and fine 96×64×64 windows read crops of a common state. Overlap velocities are added with uniform weight and divided by local coverage at **each predictor and corrector**, with 32 Heun steps and 64 network evaluations per window per stage. The fine state is projected back onto its block constraint during integration. The method reports zero-coverage cells explicitly, uses the training-only conditioned exterior with 32-cell padding, then applies the free-space cell-average potential Hessian without a second R7 smoothing. Truth uses the full periodic simulation box. Both use ascending eigenvalues, strict threshold 0.2 and positive-overdensity trace convention. Blind truth verifies trace against density contrast to 2×10⁻⁶; the indexed blind prediction receipts record worst trace residual 1.49×10⁻¹³ or smaller. The true-density/learned-exterior control retains a substantial share of the regional shear-spread offset. No evidence justifies changing the threshold or correcting posterior draws after scoring.

Engineering tests establish identical overlap values and single-window agreement with patch integration. They cannot establish the right joint law. The two caps have independent noise and external completions. Cross-cap correlations, exterior short-scale structure, survey-wide isotropy and calibrated connectivity cannot be inferred from these checks.

Local marginal flows can have all the observed data and still lack the other cells of the evolving stochastic state. Their overlap-averaged velocity need not equal the global velocity. Exact block updates conditional on the sampled exterior preserve the toy target, giving a constructive control. ## The spatial measurement and the counterfactual

Let F(x) equal one if exactly two of the three eigenvalues exceed 0.2 at x. For a particular truth or ensemble realization and lag d, form the multiset P of ordered endpoint pairs (x,x+d eᵢ) within one 32×16×16 grid region over the three positive Cartesian axes. Both endpoints must satisfy positive native random support and lie in the registered redshift bins. No periodic wrap, inter-region or cross-cap pairs enter. Intermediate cells need not be supported. The grid spacing is 6.766 h⁻¹ Mpc, so the three separations are 6.766, 13.532 and 27.064 h⁻¹ Mpc. The functional is

**T = Σₚ F(left)F(right)/N − (Σₚ F(left)/N)(Σₚ F(right)/N), with N = |P|.**

The axis pools contribute in proportion to their numbers of supported pairs. Endpoint means are separately pair-weighted; one cell can appear in multiple pairs. Every field including truth gets the *same* functional of its own realized endpoints. There is no model-fitted or ensemble-mean centering. T is a within-realization regional spatial covariance. Its distribution over draws is the forecast. It measures neither the posterior covariance of two fixed voxels nor paths along connected filaments. The code has no empty-pair handling and comparison with NaN would silently give false filament votes. The replay found finite supported predictions and nonempty pair sets in every scored region. `lowz_spatial_venue.py` lines 25–32 and 37–58.

The historical shuffle permutes the 32 Boolean filament indicators independently at each voxel. Exact count equality proves equality of the whole Bernoulli empirical law, probability, empirical and fair Brier and one-cell Bernoulli CRPS. The replay explicitly tested count equality and squared probability errors before aggregation. The spatially varying probability map survives. Dependence of different cells across a realization is what is removed in expectation; individual shuffled draws and finite ensembles retain spatial variation. Shuffling density then recomputing nonlocal tides would answer another question.

The reference uses log(1 + Gaussian-smoothed CIC counts / floored smoothed support), redshift, smoothed support and polynomial/interacting terms (12 coefficients per target). Ridge uses at most 2,048 supported cells per training region, 16 regions per phase, penalty 0.0001 times the sampled row count. The spatial experiment subtracts the **full 20-phase fit** from every training-region transformed target, preserving the actual anisotropic residual field without fitting a correlation length. Add a complete donor region to the recipient’s own regression prediction and invert the log density / middle eigenvalue / log-gap transform. Donors match cap, nominal shell/support stratum and replicate name, on the same local lattice. They do not enforce identical donor/recipient masks, detailed observing conditions or global cap continuity. Only 80 of 320 donor–recipient matches per development phase had identical stored centres and grid origins. The recipient support determines scoring. The original 32 donors are sampled independently with replacement **per region**; 13–19 distinct donors occurred. Baseline amplitudes and correlations therefore derive from an in-sample, finite regional library. This is a credible and inexpensive local null with fewer observational channels than the CFM. It is distinct from the pointwise out-of-fold marginal residual reference. `lowz_count_reference.py` lines 22–56, 61–99 and `lowz_spatial_venue.py` lines 15–22, 49–55.

For the matched control use all 20 donors exactly once. For ordered λ₁ ≤ λ₂ ≤ λ₃, filament means λ₁ ≤ 0.2 **and** λ₂ > 0.2. Rank members by min(λ₂−0.2, 0.2−λ₁). Positive margins are filaments, negative margins are nonfilaments. At zero, λ₁=0.2<λ₂ is a filament, while λ₂=0.2 is not. Break equal margins by actual membership then stable member order. For baseline member j of rank k at that cell, assign the *entire* kth CFM tuple. This preserves every empirical within-cell joint distribution, including class probability and continuous component scores; inverse-permutation equality and exact filament counts passed on every rearrangement. The result borrows the **rank dependence of the baseline filament margin**; it need not reproduce the baseline’s binary correlations exactly once occurrence frequencies change. Other scalar projections have different rank dependence. The rearrangement breaks the nonlocal density–tide relationship across cells and is only a diagnostic.

### Scoring and calibration

For sorted or unsorted statistic values vᵢ and truth y, empirical CRPS is mean |vᵢ−y| minus Σᵢⱼ|vᵢ−vⱼ|/(2M²). Fair CRPS substitutes 2M(M−1) in the denominator. The saved comparator implements the pair sum directly. We independently rescored by the sorted order-statistic identity. Conditional on fixed model/observation and independent noise draws, the correction estimates the continuous-model score. With independent sampling with replacement from a frozen finite donor library it estimates that library’s score, even if sampled indices repeat. Enumerating every donor is a discrete forecast in its own right. ECC and a cellwise permutation couple members through shared ranks or without-replacement constraints. Calling the corrected formula unbiased for a latent generator in these arms would require extra assumptions. Matched-size empirical CRPS is primary; fair-formula results and subset variation are provided for all methods. [Fair ensemble scores](https://doi.org/10.1002/qj.2270)

Intervals are NumPy linear 5th–95th percentiles with endpoints included. In the original 32-draw run the interpolation positions are 1.55 and 29.45 (zero based). There is no exact distribution-free 90% coverage for those interpolated endpoints. For continuous exchangeable draws, the interval from order statistics k to l has expected coverage (l−k)/(M+1). The accompanying third through (M−2)nd order interval gives 27/33 at M=32 and 15/21 at M=20. T is discrete on a finite lattice, so ties also matter. Files report strict-less rank, tie count and both coverages; uniform-rank interpretation is conditional on exchangeability and tie treatment. Correlated regions, overlapping subsets and three lag measurements cannot be counted as independent simulations. [Diagnostic test quantities](https://doi.org/10.1214/23-BA1404)

Original 32-draw one-cell CFM results by ph006/012/013 had mean truth .04852/.04926/.05267, draw mean .04526/.04790/.04770, signed error −.00326/−.00136/−.00497, mean width .02039/.02109/.02084, mean truth rank 19.81/19.31/23.31 of 32, and coverage 14/16, 14/16, 10/16. On ph013 five truths exceeded the upper endpoint and one was below the lower endpoint. Regional RMS centre error was .00885 versus RMS ensemble SD .00737. Removing the phase-average offset descriptively gives a centred RMS near .00733. The direction and magnitude point chiefly to a low centre on this phase. Some regions have insufficient spread relative to their realized error. One truth per region cannot identify conditional dispersion separately from regional centre errors. A wider template improves ph013 coverage even while shifting the mean further downward. Claims of uniformly sharp, calibrated intervals or a universal dispersion defect would exceed the evidence. These numbers are equal-region development estimates at [0.01,0.55), original `spatial_venue_62121276`. Detailed reporting and generalization follow below.

Peak selection uses the maximum support-corrected smoothed-count feature among centres whose entire 5³ neighborhood passes the science mask. One such centre per registered region was available. It is observation-selected, not an identified halo or necessarily a catalogue galaxy. Draw–draw distance uses the 496 distinct unordered pairs from 32 draws, excluding the diagonal. Observed positions improve marginal prediction relative to a local model that already receives galaxy information. That contrast does not isolate how much *coupling* is learned from the particular observed galaxy pattern. All four controls inherit the target cell's CFM marginal or count prediction; the rank experiment deliberately preserves much of that information. A future targeted conditioning control could pair different mock phases at the same grid position with closely matched survey response and radial selection, keeping complete, internally consistent observation tensors together. Compare a region's truth under its own observations versus the other phase's real observations with matching geometry. Rotating only counts against a fixed mask or deleting a trained channel would introduce a different distribution shift. Such a paired comparison addresses the value of conditioning; separating conditional marginals and dependence would still require a common-marginal rank experiment. It would not establish architectural causality by itself.

A mechanism paper could instead contrast shared integration with independent-window integration at the same fixed weights and coarse draw, using **one** predeclared cross-window enclosed-mass or unaligned subvolume mean and matched interior windows of comparable support. Coarse-block-aligned mass is fixed by construction, making it an unsuitable fine-stage diagnostic. No such matched ablation is in the current low-z evidence. It is unnecessary for the present publication decision.

I found no exact published duplicate of the combination of a DESI BGS cap model, positive coarse-mass-conserving generation, explicit exterior tides and a held-out rank-controlled filament score. That is a qualified literature judgment, not a priority claim. The strongest present ML framing is **B**, empirical scientific value, with a bounded diagnostic element of **C**. Architecture **A** is credible implementation evidence. As a publication the balance favors **E**. Remove claims of new copula theory, first cosmic-web posterior, first large 3D flow reconstruction, fully calibrated fields, measured global connectivity, a learned dependence advantage everywhere, or a faster substitute for BORG.

**Sceptical ICML review.** Novelty is moderate for the application and modest for the general method. The posterior construction and specified forecast scores are technically coherent. Experimental rigor is strong on frozen weights, registered rows, explicit controls and disclosure of reversals; two independent follow-up phases and one nonlinear primary observable restrict generalization. Significance to survey users is high, significance beyond this inverse problem remains uncertain. The paper becomes clearer when the unit of forecast is the realized regional covariance. Reproducibility inside the collaboration is good with hashed inputs and exact replay, but multi-stage scratch and survey-access dependencies limit external reproducibility. A small publicly releasable benchmark would improve that judgment; this audit did not publish data externally.

**Strongest rejection argument.** A simplified donor-rank template attached to the very same cell predictions obtains much of the scientific score, and the two held-out phases disagree on which dependence is preferable. The remaining comparison to independent shuffling penalizes an ensemble deliberately deprived of realistic spatial association. Related work already learns cosmic fields, propagates them to observables and detects underdispersion. The paper therefore demonstrates a valuable survey product, while leaving unclear what another inference team should change about its learning algorithm.

The matched control directly answers the empirical part of that objection. It supplies a transferable *evaluation practice*: put physically motivated rank dependence on identical marginals and score the derived quantity users will measure. It supplies weak support for a transferable *learning* advance. The follow-up also limits a negative-result interpretation: there is no consistent learned-coupling failure across phases, and an alternative can be wider yet score better or worse. The conditional marginal and power diagnostics could not have discriminated these counterfactuals by construction. A substantially replicated failure with a mechanistic explanation or a matched boundary-spanning observation showing a distinctive advantage could reopen a main-track case. Another precision estimate on the same two phases cannot supply that evidence. No further experiment is needed to choose the current venues.
