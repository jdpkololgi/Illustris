## T-web definition

Forero-Romero et al., A Dynamical Classification of the Cosmic Web (2009).
https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.4135
https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/396/3/1815/1747417

Search result/abstract confirms classification by number of tidal eigenvalues above a threshold: zero, one, two and three correspond to void, sheet, filament and knot. Supports replacing the slide's density-ordering arrow with a threshold-count explanation. The positive threshold has dynamical motivation; do not describe one chosen value as a universal exact collapse criterion.

## TARP

Lemos, Coogan, Hezaveh & Perreault-Levasseur, Sampling-Based Accuracy Testing of Posterior Estimators for General Inference (2023).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.03026
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/lemos23a/lemos23a.pdf

Search result/abstract describes Tests of Accuracy with Random Points as coverage testing for generative posterior estimators, including high-dimensional applications. Its theoretical result must not be equated with proof from a single finite-sample near-diagonal plot. The current slide's sample size and bootstrap-envelope description were read from the deck, not established by this reference.
